`agentic-helpers bound` is built in agentic-helpers commit c1373f2, not yet released, closing the directive (20261030-160759-d-tac-7ep): where `timeout` is missing, shell-guard rewrites `timeout N cmd` to `agentic-helpers bound N cmd` alone.

**Built.** `internal/bound` runs the command in its own process group, sends the group `SIGTERM` at the bound and `SIGKILL` 5 seconds later, forwards `SIGINT`, `SIGTERM` and `SIGHUP`, and exits 124 on expiry, the command's own status otherwise, 125 for its own bad arguments, 126 or 127 when the command cannot run or is not found. `cmd/agentic-helpers` dispatches `bound` before any migration or settings read. In `internal/alternatives` an alternative gains `Subcommand` and `Bundled` (offered without a `PATH` lookup, since a hook's `PATH` need not hold the plugin's `bin/`), and an entry gains `Walked`: for `timeout`, `timeout` itself, `gtimeout`, `uu-timeout` and the perl alarm form, which the walk still reads and a rewrite no longer offers. `internal/shellwalk` reads a launcher with a subcommand only where that subcommand follows as fixed text.

**Tests**, each guard broken and caught:
1. The test binary as a Go child sleeping 10 minutes ends at a 1-second bound with 124; a child exiting 3 keeps 3; a zero duration bounds nothing.
2. A child ignoring `SIGTERM` is killed after the grace; a grandchild ends with the group.
3. A `SIGTERM` sent to the bound reaches the command, which ends with 143.
4. A bad duration or an option before it exits 125, a missing command 127; durations parse as GNU writes them, refusing a sign, an exponent, hex and `inf`.
5. Without `timeout`, the rewrite names `agentic-helpers bound` whether or not `gtimeout`, `uu-timeout` or perl are present; with it, nothing is refused.
6. `agentic-helpers bound 10 rm -rf ~/x` asks as outside the project, also by its path, and `./build` runs; a split duration or `sudo` in front take the lone-removal verdict as `timeout` does; `agentic-helpers check rm -rf ~/x` is no launcher.
7. The skill test quotes the new rewrite and 124; the machine line reads `timeout -> agentic-helpers bound`.

**Proven live** on the installed build: a Go program sleeping 6 seconds ended after 2 with exit 124, `sh -c 'exit 3'` exited 3, a missing command 127, a bad duration 125; fed hook input with a `PATH` lacking `timeout`, shell-guard denied `timeout 9 make test` with `use: agentic-helpers bound 9 make test`, kept a pipeline in `use: agentic-helpers bound 5m go test ./... | tail -5`, asked for `agentic-helpers bound 9 rm -rf ~/x` as outside the project and allowed `./build`; `agentic-helpers check` printed `this machine: timeout -> agentic-helpers bound; sed and date are BSD`.

**Deviation.** A duration built at run time (`timeout "$d" make`) is rewritten exactly, `agentic-helpers bound "$d" make`, since `bound` takes the word as it stands; the directive listed it among the forms answered with slots. Only options (`-s`, `-k`) get the slotted form.

The agent-shell-guard skill, `docs/shell-guard/`, `docs/plugin/usage.md` and `AGENTS.md` changed in the same commit.
