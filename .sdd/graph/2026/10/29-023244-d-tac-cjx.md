`agentic-memory who` and `who --all` leave out free seats that filed nothing and had nothing used in 14 days, and end each project's block with one count line naming the flag that shows them: `+<n> unused seats: agentic-memory who --unused`. `who --unused` lists them as well.

- **Unused** means the row's `filed 0 / used 0 (14d)`, the counts of 20261026-222129-d-tac-ak9, on a free seat.
- **Still listed:** held seats, and free seats with any filing or use in the window.
- **Unchanged:** `who --json` and the MCP `who` tool list every seat.
- The `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` skill (its seat section and `who` line) and `docs/memory/` usage change with it.

Why: `who --all`, the listing of every seat that 20261025-232221-d-tac-jg4 set, printed 101 seats in 121 lines and 7.3k characters: 6 held, 39 free but active, 56 free and unused for 14 days, mostly finished autopilot board-row seats (one per row and role). Every seat ever claimed stays listed (`source/hooks/internal/memory/who.go`).

Rejected: `who --all` showing held seats only (busy free seats in other projects, such as fdbox's main and idea, would vanish); deleting unused free seats (name and id gone, and a board reusing a seat name would get a fresh one).

Accepted cost: an unused seat is one flag away.

Acceptance: a free seat with `filed 0 / used 0` is absent from `who` and `who --all` and counted in the line; `--unused` lists it; a held seat with nothing filed still shows; `--json` lists every seat.
