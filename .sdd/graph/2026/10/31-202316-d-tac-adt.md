A fresh session that takes over a seat a launcher reserved for it (`agentic-memory claim <seat> --session <id>`, 20261025-223640-d-tac-d2p) starts with that seat's scratch folder created, the `scratch: <folder>` line and the seat's brief, as a direct claim gives them. Until now the session took the seat over silently: SessionStart adopts the reservation, but prints the scratch line and the brief only after a compaction or `/clear`, so a launched worker started without the user's rules, the seat's traps and state, or a named place for throwaway files. The reservation exists so a launched job is seated from its first moment without depending on the agent following its brief; a seat it cannot see defeats that.

**Scope.** Only a session starting fresh (`SessionStart` source `startup`) that adopts a reservation. A resumed session keeps printing nothing, since its transcript already carries what it saw. A reservation taken over at the first prompt or tool call instead of at SessionStart prints the same at that hook. No user overview either way: a launcher's claim is still no request from the user. The scratch folder is `.tmp/seats/<seat>/` (20261031-175124-d-tac-vw1).

**Rejected.** The scratch line without the brief: the launcher would have to pass the user's rules in its prompt, and a launcher that forgets leaves the worker blind. Documenting the silence as intended: launched workers would keep writing throwaway files with no folder named and acting without the rules.

**Accepted cost.** A launched worker's first context grows by the brief, up to its 9,000-character cap.

**Acceptance.**
- A session started on a reservation gets `.tmp/seats/<seat>/` created, the `scratch:` line and the brief at SessionStart; a test drives it.
- A resumed session on its seat still prints neither.
- `docs/memory/usage.md` states what a session taking over a reservation receives.
