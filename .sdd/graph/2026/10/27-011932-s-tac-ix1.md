Every agentic-memory entry is filed with a kind (rule, trap, todo, note) that sets its decay and brief section, and each seat holds one overwritable state slot, in agentic-helpers commit 3c7ed93 with a missing test added in 0d6fdab, not yet released, completing 20261026-215042-d-tac-g5a. The store moves to format 4.

Each criterion with its test in `internal/memory/`:

1. Each kind files with its decay; `--retention` is refused naming `--kind`, CLI and MCP: TestNewFilesEachKind, TestEachKindSetsItsDecay, TestRetentionIsRefusedNamingKind.
2. A rule without `--from user`, and `rekind` to rule of such an entry, are refused: TestARuleNeedsTheUser, TestRekind.
3. `state` overwrites the slot, the brief opens with it and its age, and the slot never appears in `search`, the live count or recall: TestTheStateSlot, TestRecallNeverOffersTheStateSlot.
4. A state write moves the replay anchor, answers the compaction nudge and re-arms the replay nudge: three tests in `kinds_test.go`.
5. The brief's section order: TestTheBriefOrdersItsSections.
6. A format-3 store migrates under lock, standing to rule, the rest to note keeping retention as an override, with a byte-equal backup: TestAFormat3StoreMigratesToKinds, TestTwoCallsMigrateAFormat3StoreOnce, TestAssignKinds.
7. The horizon fold keeps each seat's newest state row: TestAMergeKeepsEachSeatsNewestStateRow.
8. The docs and agentic-helpers:agent-memory name the kinds, the slot, `rekind` and the new nudge wording.

Binaries before this change cannot read a migrated store.
