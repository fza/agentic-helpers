Done for 20261025-153956-d-tac-zbb: commit 4e6b29a, released as v2.3.0 (release commit e355cfc).

- `retire --because '<why>'` (MCP key `because`) writes `{"because": ...}` into the retire event for every id; search lines, `show` and the event history print `retired <stamp>: <why>`; a payload-less retire from an earlier release reads as a retire without a reason; `revive` takes the reason with it.
- `--term` matches the reason of the entry's standing retire or supersede beside title and body.
- Tests: `TestARetireCarriesItsReason`, `TestARetireFromAnEarlierReleaseReadsWithoutAReason`; the reason folded, printed, searched and written were each broken and caught.
- Deviation: only the filing seat retires its entries, so another seat's retire can never reach the brief's changes by other seats; that half of AC 2 has no case. A status check limiting the searched reason to retires was removed, no test being able to fail it.
