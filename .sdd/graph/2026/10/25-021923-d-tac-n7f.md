**Every stamp agentic-memory prints names its zone**: `2026-10-25 02:35 CEST`, in entry lines, `show`, the brief and event histories, in the local zone. Why: a local time alone repeats an hour each autumn when the clocks go back, so two entries from that hour read out of order. Heat, decay and every comparison keep running on the stored UTC time.

**A search warms its top three results only**, `+0.25` each, where every hit was warmed before. Why: a broad search returning most of the store (`--term .`) says nothing about any one entry, and warming every hit levels the working set, pushing the freshest entries to the bottom of the brief. Three matches the cut prompt recall makes.

Rejected: UTC stamps, unambiguous but off by an hour or two from every other clock the user reads; warming no search hit at all, which loses the signal a narrow search carries.

Accepted cost: entry lines grow by the zone's abbreviation.
