agentic-memory tells the agent of a note or todo at the tool call that touches a path the entry declares with `--about`, once per session, as traps are told at their tool calls (20261026-220335-d-tac-nu3).

Why: notes and todos reach a session only through the brief and per-prompt recall, both keyed to the claim or the prompt's words, and a seat's notes go unread: one seat filed 90 entries and used 1 in 14 days. A note about a file matters at the moment the agent works on that file, which a tool call marks.

What:

- **Declared paths only.** A note or todo triggers on the paths given with `--about` (MCP `about`), matched against the paths a call names as trap triggers are. The paths its title and body merely mention do not trigger: notes mention many paths, and the trap directive rejected triggering every kind on its anchors because most calls would print one. That rejection stands.
- **Notice**: `agentic-memory: about <path>: <short brief line>`, through the memory tool hook after the call, at most one notice per call (the hottest entry when several match), each entry once per session and again after a compaction, through the same seen marks traps use.
- **No heat from delivery.** Being told is not a use: a note warms only when the agent then shows, confirms or links it, as for recall.
- Entries of the seat's shelf and those received through `--for` or a followed topic both trigger, as traps do.

Rejected: every anchored path triggering, which floods calls as the trap directive found; a PreToolUse deny carrying the note, which costs a retried call per first touch; delivery counting as a use, which would warm entries nobody read and break heat as the measure of use.

Accepted costs: notes filed without `--about` never reach a tool call, so the skill asks for `--about` on a note about a file; one more match per tool call of a seated session holding such entries.

Acceptance criteria:

1. A note or todo with `--about <path>` prints its notice once per session at the first call naming that path, and again after a compaction.
2. A path mentioned only in the body never triggers.
3. At most one notice prints per call.
4. Delivery adds no heat; a following `show` does.
5. Each guard is broken once and its test watched failing; `docs/memory/` and agentic-helpers:agent-memory quote the notice and ask for `--about`, in the same change.
