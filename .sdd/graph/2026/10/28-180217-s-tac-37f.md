`agentic-capture` refuses a titled draft with `whether the graph takes entry titles is unknown: <what sdd printed>` when `sdd info --format json` fails, before lint, pre-flight or any `sdd new` runs, closing 20261028-175441-d-tac-0dn. Commit c8ddcba carries it, with `docs/capture/usage.md` and the `agentic-helpers:agent-capture` skill naming the refusal; it is not yet released.

The failed-run case of `TestSddInfoSaysWhetherTheGraphTakesTitles` runs a titled draft against an `sdd info` exiting 1 and requires the refusal to carry sdd's output, with no dry run and no capture after it; returning no refusal on that error fails the case on its own assertion.

Proven live on the `agentic-capture` built from the commit: in a fresh graph whose `.sdd/config.yaml` does not parse, `sdd info --format json` exits 1, and a titled draft was refused at once with sdd's parse error quoted and its exit code named.
