The grounding gate finds sdd, agentic-capture and gather calls by walking the bash syntax tree, in agentic-helpers commit 766072b, not yet released, completing 20261026-223947-d-tac-wtx.

- The command walk moves into `internal/shellwalk`, shared with shell-guard.
- Only calls the shell itself runs earn read credit; calls inside `bash -c`, `eval` or a heredoc fed to a shell keep the stream and depth checks but earn none.
- The command after a launcher is the first word past its options, so `xargs grep -l agentic-capture` and `find . -name agentic-capture` are not writes.
- An unparseable command naming `sdd` or `agentic-capture` as a word counts as a plain `sdd new`.

Every criterion holds with tests, the existing gate and shell-guard suites unchanged and green. Each new check was disabled in turn and its test watched failing; the decode-error fallback and printer-error branch of `shellwalk.Spelled` and a nil-redirect check cannot be reached.

Noticed in the doing: shell-guard's literal reading treats `$'rm'` as an expansion, so `$'rm' -rf` runs unjudged; unchanged here.
