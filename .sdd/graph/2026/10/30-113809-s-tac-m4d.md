Age-based pruning of tool library versions (20261030-000535-d-tac-0y1) is built in commit 47aef3b, with the key named `tools_version_lifetime` and read as a span (default `90d`, at least `14d`) as the span decision (20261030-084036-d-tac-e9g) renamed it.

**Live proof** on the installed build, against a throwaway home: a tool with v1 (a fork's origin), v2 and v3, with the time v3 replaced v2 set past 90 days in the store's history. The SessionStart hook removed `v2/`, wrote `pruned.jnl`, and kept v1, the origin, and v3, the current version.

**Suites** in `source/hooks`: `TestTheSessionStartPassPrunesVersionsReplacedPastTheirLifetime` (91 days pruned, current and origin kept), `TestAVersionWithNoReadableReplacementKeepsItsPlace`, `TestAPrunedVersionStillRefusesItsFilesAsADuplicate` (the shared `pruned.jnl` path), and the `pluginconfig` cases holding the key to `config.yaml` with a 14-day floor. README, `docs/tools/usage.md`, `docs/tools/architecture.md`, `docs/plugin/usage.md` and the agent-tools skill name the key and the rule.
