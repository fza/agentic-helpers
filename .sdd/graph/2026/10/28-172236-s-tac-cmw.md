`agentic-capture`'s header check requires `confidence` beside `type` and `layer`, refusing a draft that names none with `draft refused: the leading block needs a confidence` before lint, pre-flight or any `sdd new` runs, closing 20261028-171800-s-tac-6ka. This is the gap's candidate remedy, taken without change. Commit 6300f15 carries it with its tests, `docs/capture/usage.md` and the `agentic-helpers:agent-capture` skill; it is not yet released.

A test refuses a draft without `confidence` and checks no command ran; dropping `confidence` from the required keys fails it on that assertion. Every capture fixture meant to pass the header check now names a confidence.

Proven live on the `agentic-capture` built from the commit: in a fresh schema-1 graph, a draft naming no `confidence`, run without `--skip-preflight`, was refused at once with that line, with no pre-flight and no `sdd new` help.
