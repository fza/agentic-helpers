The memory MCP server's `search`, `brief`, `view` and `who` answer their CLI text as `message`, in agentic-helpers commit 373f384, not yet released; `show` and `topics` keep their JSON.

Proven live on the `~/go/bin` build against the real store: `search`, `brief --kind rule`, `view` and `who` answered `message` alone, with scores and `(seen)` pointers in the text; `show` answered `entries` and `notice`, `topics` its `topics` object.

Deviation: MCP `who` gained an `unused` key. Its text only counts seats with nothing filed or used (`+<n> unused seats: agentic-memory who --unused`), and the JSON it replaced listed them, so without the key an agent on MCP could no longer reach them.

Criteria: 1 holds, guarded by `TestReadsAnswerTheirTextOverMCP` (each of the four, message equal to the CLI text) and `TestEveryToolAnswersItsShape`, which now also asserts that `show` and `topics` answer JSON: the schema the shape check reads comes from the same list as the code, so switching `show` to text passed until that independent assertion was added. 2 holds: the `--json` tests run unchanged on the CLI. 3 holds: the text the MCP answer equals carries the seen pointers, scores and `more:` hints. 4 holds: `docs/memory/usage.md`, `architecture.md` and the skill's `reference.md`. 5 holds: putting each of the four back on JSON, and `show` or `topics` on text, fails its test.
