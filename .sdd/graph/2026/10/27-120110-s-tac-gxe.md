`agentic-capture` refuses a draft carrying `title` on a graph whose `.sdd/meta.json` holds `graph_schema_version: 1`, before the lint, the pre-flight or any `sdd new` runs, exiting 1 with ``the graph is on schema 1, which has no entry titles; drop the draft's `title` key, or run `sdd migrate` to upgrade the graph``. It landed in agentic-helpers commit b143ccf, not yet released, and closes the early title refusal directive (20261027-115753-d-tac-swc).

`TestASchemaOneGraphTakesNoTitle` covers:

- a titled draft on schema 1: exit 1, stderr naming the missing titles and `sdd migrate`, only the `--help` probes run;
- an untitled draft on schema 1: captured;
- a titled draft on a graph naming no schema: captured.

The guard was broken and its case watched failing, then restored byte-identically; `go vet` and `go test -race ./...` in `source/hooks` pass. `agentic-helpers:agent-capture`, `docs/capture/usage.md` and `docs/capture/architecture.md` name the refusal.
