The cosine floor agentic-tools' `find --query` and per-prompt offer hold a tool's description to becomes the plugin option `tools_query_floor`, 0.45 unless set, in place of the fixed 0.6.

A cosine's range depends on the embedder. With `embeddinggemma-300m-6bit`, natural paraphrases of a registered tool's purpose scored 0.50 to 0.63 against it and unrelated needs 0.35 to 0.38, so a floor of 0.6 dropped most real searches and would rarely let the offer fire; a query close to the description's own words scored 0.83.

- **Plugin option `tools_query_floor`**, a number above 0 and at most 1, read as `tools_duplicate_floor` is, and declared in the plugin's `userConfig`. It applies before the take and own-project lifts, as the fixed floor did, to `find --query` and to the offer alike.
- **0.45 by default**, between the unrelated needs and the weakest paraphrase measured; a machine on another embedder sets its own.

Why: the floor's right value belongs to the embedder, which the machine's settings already name, so it sits beside the embedding options and the duplicate floor rather than in the code.

Rejected:
- A lower constant in place of 0.6: right for one embedder, wrong for the next.
- A `--floor` flag on `find`: an agent would tune it per search, and the offer has no caller to pass one.
- Separate floors for `find` and the offer: one measurement backs both, and the offer pushes names only, so a weak match costs a few tokens.

Accepted costs: 0.45 rests on one tool and a handful of queries, so the default may move as the library grows; a match just above the floor can be loosely related.

Acceptance criteria:
1. With no option set, `find --query` and the offer keep a tool whose cosine lies at or above 0.45 and drop one below it.
2. `tools_query_floor` set in the plugin's options moves both; a value outside (0, 1] is refused as the settings' parse error, as `tools_duplicate_floor`'s is.
3. `plugin/.claude-plugin/plugin.json` declares the option; `docs/tools/usage.md`, `docs/tools/architecture.md` and `agentic-helpers:agent-tools` name it and its default.
