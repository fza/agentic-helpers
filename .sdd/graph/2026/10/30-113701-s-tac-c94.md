Seat-named scratch folders (20261030-082426-d-tac-h68) and the claim creating `.tmp/` (20261030-093410-d-tac-qks) are built in commit 57d36ee and proven on the installed build against a throwaway home and a fresh git checkout without `.tmp/`.

**Proof.**
- `agentic-memory claim probe` in the fresh checkout created `.tmp/`, then `.tmp/claude/probe/` holding the marker `.agentic-scratch` with the seat's id, and printed `scratch: .tmp/claude/probe/`. git ignored `.tmp/` through the global excludes step.
- With `scratch_sweep` unset (`release`), `agentic-memory release probe` removed the seat folder.
- A folder at a seat's name without its marker is reported `(a keep, never swept)` at claim and survives; this repository's own `.tmp/claude/main/` shows that line at every session start.

The suites in `source/hooks` cover the rest of both acceptance lists: `TestScratchSweepDecidesWhenTheSeatsFolderGoes` (the `session` and `idle` modes), `TestTheSessionStartReapsAWeekIdleSeatFolder` and `TestTheReapTakesOnlyAWeekIdleFolderOfAKnownSeat` (the 7-day reap), `TestAKeepAtTheSeatsNameIsNeverSwept`, `TestOldSessionFoldersMoveIntoTheirSeatsFolder` and `TestATranscriptNamesTheSeatOfAnOlderSessionFolder` (the migration), `TestAClaimGivesAGitCheckoutItsTmpFolder` and `TestNoClaimCreatesTmpWithScratchOffOrOutsideGit`. The agent-scratch skill, `docs/scratch/` and `docs/memory/usage.md` describe the layout, the option and the migration.
