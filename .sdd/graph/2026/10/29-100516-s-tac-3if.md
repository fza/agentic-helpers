Machine-wide size limits for agentic-memory attachments and the agentic-tools library are built, with the brief's space line limited to stores under 10% of their limit left, completing the space-line directive (20261029-084411-d-tac-pi9) and all of the size-limits directive (20261029-024444-d-tac-x9j) but one criterion. Commits 3b80014 through 4f6184f on main carry it with tests for every criterion built, a new `internal/space` ledger, and the changes to the `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` and `agentic-helpers:agent-tools` skills, README `Options` and `docs/`, not yet released.

**Live on the installed binaries:** an `attach` reply ended `space left: attachments 2.0 GB of 2.0 GB`; an `agentic-tools add` reply in a throwaway home ended `space left: tools 500.0 MB of 500.0 MB`; a 1.1 MB tool was refused as holding more than the 1 MB a tool may hold; the brief carried no space line with both stores far from their limits; and `view 'active:attached:rank(size):as-list'` listed each file's size and the total.

**Deviations from x9j.**
- *Criterion 5, `tools_total_mb` in `config.yaml` only:* not built, since the `config.yaml` layering of 20261028-212647-d-tac-cld does not exist yet. `tools_total_mb` is declared as a plugin option beside `attachments_total_mb` and takes effect there; x9j stays open for this criterion until that layering lands.
- *Criterion 9, no directory walk:* every figure shown comes from recorded sizes, but a prune is decided only on sizes measured on disk (an lstat of the version's files), and only when the recorded figures say a store is over its limit. Recorded index and ledger figures are not trusted to delete anything, so a corrupt or forged figure cannot set off pruning in another project's store.

Beyond x9j, `agentic-tools` gained a `help` verb, the `--help` place criterion 10 names for the cap and limit text.
