Agentic-memory gains the end condition `session` (`new --until session`, MCP `until: ["session"]`): the entry retires with `end reached: session` when the hold it was filed under closes. It adds a form beside those of 20261026-215617-d-tac-45q.

- **Hold** = the filing seat's current claim (`seat.Claim` in `source/hooks/internal/seat/seat.go`: the seat id and its `claimed` stamp). Filing with `--until session` stores that pair on the entry.
- **Ended** once that claim carries `closed` (release, the SessionEnd hook, or a dead holder freed by `who` or the next claim), or the seat's current claim is a different one.
- **Not the session id.** `/clear` gives a new session id while the seat and its claim stay; `/clear` and `/resume` must not end the entry, as they do not end a shelf's lifetime. A fork that takes over a gone parent's seat (20261028-105243-d-tac-n3q) starts a new claim, so the parent's session entries have already ended.
- **Like every `--until`:** the entry stays searchable, `revive` brings it back within 14 days, and it is listed under `ended since last brief`. Not allowed on a rule. Filing with no seat is refused as any write is.
- **Code:** `parseCondition` in `ends.go` and the session-end hook in `hooks.go`; the skill's `--until` list and `docs/memory/` usage change with it.

Why: scratch findings that matter only while one session works should end with that session without the agent remembering to retire them, and the claim is the one handle that survives `/clear` and `/resume` yet closes when the session does.

Rejected: a fifth entry kind `scratch` (it could not combine with todo or trap, and `--until` already covers ends); keying on the session id (`/clear` would end the entry).

Accepted costs: a session resumed after its hold ended finds the entry retired and revives it; a crashed session counts as ended once its seat is freed.

Acceptance: a `--until session` entry retires at release and at session end; it survives `/clear` (new session id, same claim); another claim's entries stay untouched; `revive` brings it back.
