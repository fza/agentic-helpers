Where `sdd info --help` lists `--format` and `sdd info --format json` then fails or prints no readable JSON, `agentic-capture` refuses a titled draft before lint, pre-flight or any `sdd new` runs, with `whether the graph takes entry titles is unknown: <what sdd printed>`. A draft without `title` never asks `sdd info` and is unaffected.

Why: an sdd that lists the flag is a release whose answer outranks the `.sdd/meta.json` read, and that read says no to schema-1 graphs whose writes take titles on fork release fza.32. Refusing names the real fault, sdd's own output, where a guess would either refuse a capturable draft for the wrong reason or pass one sdd may reject.

Rejected:

- Falling back to the `.sdd/meta.json` read: it refuses titles on schema-1 graphs that take them, so a passing `sdd info` and a failing one would answer the same graph differently.
- Passing the title to `sdd new` and letting sdd judge: sdd's refusal then arrives after the pre-flight has run.

Accepted cost: a titled capture fails whenever `sdd info` fails, even on a graph that takes titles; dropping the `title` key captures it.

Done when a test runs a titled draft against a failing `sdd info` and sees the refusal naming what sdd printed with no command after it, the guard broken and watched failing, and `docs/capture/usage.md` names the refusal.
