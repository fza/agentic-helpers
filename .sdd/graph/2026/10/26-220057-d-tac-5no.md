The agentic-memory brief and per-prompt recall print a short line, notes in the brief are capped, and inbound link lists skip superseded entries.

Why: the brief of the agentic-memory directive (20261025-014326-d-tac-mvn) prints at every claim and every compaction, so each field on its lines is paid for every time. A heavy seat's brief held about 50 working lines, many dead, each carrying seat, retention, source, a full stamped time and a commit that an agent reading the brief rarely uses. A `show` of one entry listed seven inbound links, the backlinks of the linked-entries directive (20261025-180343-d-tac-p9f), from state entries that had each superseded the one before.

Short line, brief and recall only:

- `<id> <title>  [<age>[, <seat>][, retain <level>]]  <flags>`, for example `m-h8pdp8 fdbox guard inventory  [20h]  ← 1`.
- `<age>` is the time since filing in the largest whole unit: `m`, `h`, `d`, `w`.
- `<seat>` appears only when the filing seat is not the reader's.
- `retain <level>` appears only when a `retain` override differs from the decay the entry's kind sets.
- Flags are unchanged: status, `drifted:`, `gone:`, `other branch:`, link flags, `← n`.
- `search`, `show` and `show`'s `linked from:` keep the full line.
- `from` drops from the short line: rules are always from the user, and the section header names the kind (20261026-215042-d-tac-g5a).

Notes cap:

- The brief prints the hottest notes up to the plugin option `brief_note_limit` (default 10), then `+<n> more notes: agentic-memory brief --kind note`.
- Rules keep their cap of a third of the budget. Traps and todos print whole: traps are rare, and todos end through their declared conditions (20261026-215617-d-tac-45q).
- The 9000-character budget stays as the outer limit, with its footer.
- `brief --kind <kind>` (MCP `kind`) lists one kind's live entries whole, as `brief --topic` lists one topic's.

Inbound links:

- `show`'s `linked from:`, the `← n` count and the brief's and recall's citing lines leave out superseded entries, as `search` does.
- `show --with-superseded` (MCP `with_superseded`) lists them.
- Retired citing entries stay listed: they ended, and nothing replaced them.

Rejected alternatives:

- The short line everywhere, `search` included: two hits with similar titles are told apart by filing time and commit, so every pick would cost a `show`.
- Keeping the full line: about 40% of every brief line stays spent on fields an agent rarely reads.
- One cap across traps, todos and notes: kinds compete for slots again, and a hot run of notes pushes a trap out.
- The character budget alone: a shelf with many live notes fills the brief to the budget at every claim and compaction.
- Showing only the newest entry of each supersede chain under `linked from:`: needs chain walking for a case the superseded filter already covers, since every older link in a chain is superseded.

Accepted costs:

- A breaking change to the brief's and recall's text; agentic-helpers:agent-memory and anything quoting a brief line follow in the same change.
- An agent wanting a filing commit or time from the brief runs `show`.
- A cold note reaches the agent only through search, prompt recall or `brief --kind note`.
- A new plugin option, `brief_note_limit`.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Brief and recall lines take the short form, with the seat only for another seat's entry and the override only when it differs, checked by a table-driven test.
2. `search` and `show` lines are unchanged, checked by the existing tests passing unchanged.
3. With more live notes than `brief_note_limit`, the brief prints the hottest up to the limit and the `+<n> more notes` footer; traps and todos print whole.
4. `brief --kind <kind>` lists that kind's live entries whole, CLI and MCP.
5. `show`'s `linked from:`, `← n` and citing lines leave out superseded entries; `show --with-superseded` lists them.
6. `brief_note_limit` is declared in `plugin/.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and named in docs and the skill.
