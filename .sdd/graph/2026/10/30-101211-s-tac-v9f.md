The span grammar for every agentic-helpers time option is built in agentic-helpers commit 98714bf, not yet released, closing the directive (20261030-084036-d-tac-e9g).

One reader parses `0`, a whole count with `s`, `m`, `h`, `d` or `w`, or a Go duration such as `1m0s`, and refuses anything else. It lives in its own package, `internal/span`, rather than inside `internal/pluginconfig` as the decision names: `internal/home` and `internal/memory` read spans too, and the option reader imports both. `internal/home/grace.go`, `internal/memory/lifetime.go`, `internal/embed/source.go` and the option reader all call it, and no other span grammar remains. `retired_body_days` became `retired_body_lifetime` and `tools_version_lifetime` was built as a span. `shell_guard_ask_timeout` takes a span, a bare number still counting seconds, clamped to at most `1h`. README `Options`, `docs/plugin/usage.md` and the skills name the new keys.

Proof: the span package's table test and the option reader's tests cover every form, the old key, the renamed key winning and the clamp. Live on the installed build, a `config.yaml` holding `retired_body_days: 30` and `shell_guard_ask_timeout: 120` read as `retired_body_lifetime 30d` and `shell_guard_ask_timeout 2m`. The session-start check printed `agentic-helpers: retired_body_days is now retired_body_lifetime (30d); rename it in config.yaml`.
