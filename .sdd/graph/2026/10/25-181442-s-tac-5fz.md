Done for 20261025-155655-d-tac-jyw: commit 0cb79aa, released as v2.3.0 (release commit e355cfc).

- `seat.Registry` keeps `seats.json`, id to `{"name", "follows"}`, written under the store's `.lock` through a temporary file; ids are `s-` and 6 unbiased characters of `0-9a-z`. Memory keeps names in its folded state and turns them into ids where events are written and seat files touched, so `rename` changes only the registry. No seat may be named in an id's shape.
- `format.json` is the store's one version; the manifest's `format` is dropped and an old one still reads. A fresh store starts at format 2.
- The first call on a format-1 store migrates it under the lock: backup, ids minted (keys already ids kept), a new log built by `memorylog.Build` with seat ids, seat files renamed, the log swapped, `seats.json`, then `format.json` last; a failure undoes every step and says so once per session; a newer format is refused with hooks silent.
- Tests: `migrate_test.go` over fixtures in `test/fixtures/memory/format-1/` (migration, a failure through a directory at `format.json.tmp`, two concurrent calls migrating once under `-race`, a newer store, `holder --json`'s shape, a migration minting nothing for ids), `TestRename`, `registry_test.go`; every guard was broken and caught, and a fresh-store guard found masked by a migration got its own assertion.
- Verified live: both stores on this machine migrated at the first 2.3.0 call, backups under `backup/format-1-*`.
- Accepted: a 2.2.x binary cannot read a migrated log, as its manifest carries no format; such sessions restart.
