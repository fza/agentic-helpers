`agentic-capture` takes a draft whose header names no `confidence` through its header check, lint and pre-flight, and only `sdd new` refuses it: sdd prints its whole `new` help and ends with `Required flag "confidence" not set`, as the live proof of 20261028-170658-s-tac-q0c showed when its draft lacked the key. The expected side: `--confidence` is the one flag `sdd new` marks required, in the fza fork (`internal/cliapp/app.go`) and in upstream sdd since commit 0f7c4add (v0.16.2), so no draft without it can ever be captured, and the header check already refuses the other inputs every capture needs (`type`, `layer`) before anything runs.

Cost: the refusal buries its one useful line under the full flag list, and a pre-flight, minutes of model time, can run first on a draft that was never capturable.

Candidate remedy, unsettled: the header check requires `confidence` beside `type` and `layer`, refusing with `the leading block needs a confidence` before lint, pre-flight or any `sdd new` runs.
