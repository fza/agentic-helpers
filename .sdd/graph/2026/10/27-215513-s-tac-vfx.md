Done for 20261027-215238-s-tac-q1n: commit 3390ff0, not yet released. Every agentic-memory claim file change now reads, judges and writes under one lock, `seats/.lock`: `claim`, `claim --session` (reservation and handover), a reservation's takeover, a hook re-pointing a claim, `release`, SessionEnd's close and `who` freeing a gone holder. `who`'s sweep and the hook rewrites change a claim only while it still names the holder they judged. A seat is minted before its claim is judged, so the seats lock is never held while the store lock is taken. The `memory` MCP server reads `seats.json` and `shelves.json` afresh on every call.

Tests in `internal/memory/claimrace_test.go` drive a live MCP server beside a second session (a seat minted, renamed and shelved after the server's first call) and race two sessions on one seat for claims, reservations, the sweep, a release naming a gone holder's seat, and a hook beside its session's release; each failed before the fix. Breaking each guard made its test fail, with two exceptions told plainly: the lock in `release` and SessionEnd's close is caught only intermittently, since its race window is too short for a test to hit reliably, and the sweep's lock is masked by its same-claim check (both broken together fail every run). A not-found check in the hook rewrite was masked by its same-holder check in every caller and was removed. `docs/memory/architecture.md` describes the lock.
