An agentic-memory store keeps one log for every shelf. Shelves are registered in a new `shelves.json` in the store root, beside `seats.json`; nothing else of the store's layout changes. A launcher gives the sessions it starts the same access to the store as any other session: it does not confine them to their own shelf.

Why: every session writing a store is trusted with all of it, as every seat is today. Confining a sandboxed job to its own shelf would take a log, lock and settings directory per shelf, a split of every store's log, quietly skipped reads and writes, and arrival rules for untrusted writers, all to hold a boundary no launcher needs to draw.

## What

- **`shelves.json`** maps shelf id (`h-` and 6 characters) to `{"name", "follows", "lifetime"}`. Each `seats.json` entry gains `"shelf": "<shelf id>"`, and its `follows` move to its shelf.
- **One log, unchanged.** An entry's shelf is its filing seat's; no event names a shelf, no event kind is added, the log is not rewritten.
- **Migration.** `format.json` rises by one. The first call on an older store, under the store lock, backs up `seats.json` to `backup/`, gives every seat a shelf of its own under its name, moves its follows there and writes `shelves.json`, as the seat-id migration (20261025-155655-d-tac-jyw) did.
- **A store's writers are trusted.** A launcher running its sessions in a sandbox grants them the store whole, `~/.claude/agentic-memory/<project>/`, or leaves them off memory.

## Rejected

- **A directory per shelf** (`shelves/<id>/` with its own log, lock, claims and settings), so a sandbox could grant a job its own shelf only. The store format and every log would split, every read would fold one log per shelf, and an action on another shelf's entry would land in the actor's log, invisible where that log is unreadable, for a confinement no launcher needs.
- **Grouping shelf directories by kind** (`shelves/temporary/`, `shelves/durable/`) so one grant follows a session moving rows. Ties a trust boundary to a lifetime: a temporary seat the owner starts by hand would sit where every job writes.
- **Reads folding only what a process can read, skipping the rest quietly.** A job's view would silently miss what durable seats did to its shelf's entries.
- **A broker outside every sandbox owning all store writes.** A long-running process for the plugin to start, supervise and secure.
- **A shelf id stamped into every event.** Rewrites the log for a mapping `seats.json` already fixes.

## Accepted costs

- A session with access to a store can file, retire or supersede on any shelf of it, and push text into other seats' sessions through arrivals (20261025-185324-s-tac-k4o); a launcher's jobs carry the owner's trust.
- The store format changes once more.

## Acceptance criteria

- An older store migrates on its first call: every seat on a shelf of its own name, its follows moved to the shelf, `shelves.json` written, `seats.json` backed up, the log untouched; brief, search and every entry line answer as before.
- `shelves.json` holds every shelf's name, follows and lifetime; each `seats.json` entry names its shelf.
- `docs/memory/architecture.md` describes `shelves.json` and the migration in the same change.
- Every new guard is broken and watched failing on its own test, the file restored byte-identically.
