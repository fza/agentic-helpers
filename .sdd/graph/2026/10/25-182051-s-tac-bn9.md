Done for 20261025-162357-d-tac-427: commit a6f619b, released as v2.3.0 (release commit e355cfc).

- `new --for <seat>` (repeatable, MCP `for`) stores the named seats by id, `--for all` stores `"for":"all"`, and a name no seat has is refused; `all` and an id's shape are refused as seat names. `follow`/`unfollow` keep a seat's topics in its `seats.json` entry, read through topic merges.
- A received entry stands with the seat's own in its brief, its default search and its prompt recall at the own seat's bar; the filer is never a recipient and alone retires it; a receiver may supersede it. A line names its recipients: `[main → all, ...]`, `[main → idea, review, ...]`, `[main → infra followers, ...]`.
- Arrivals are told once, through a seen mark in `briefs/<id>.seen.json`: the `prompt` hook adds them, a new `PostToolUse` hook `agentic-memory tool` hands them over as `additionalContext` and costs two `stat` calls where the log is unchanged, and the `Stop` hook `agentic-memory wait` (`asyncRewake`, timeout 1860) polls every 5 seconds for 30 minutes and exits 2 with the lines.
- Tests: `shared_test.go` (recipients and their lines, receiving, follows through merges, told once at prompt and tool call, the fast path writing nothing, the waiter waking and ending, a seatless session) and a recall-bar case for an entry filed for the seat; every guard was broken and caught.
- Verified live in v2.3.x: an entry the `idea` seat filed `--for main` woke this session, idle at its prompt, as a new turn reading `agentic-memory: new for main (1): m-qy3mc1 ...`; a later one arrived mid-turn through the `PostToolUse` note.
