`agentic-memory sweep` and its MCP tool are removed. `agentic-memory who` lists every seat of the current project, held or free, with its shelf, and frees the claims of the current project whose holder is gone. `who --all` lists the seats of every other project on the machine as well. MCP: `who` gains the key `all`.

Why: freeing a dead holder's claim is already automatic. A seatless session's SessionStart runs it, `claim` takes a seat whose holder is gone as free, and `holder` and `who` judge liveness at each call. What `sweep` added was the one listing of free seats, which the `agent-memory` skill reads to map a user's words onto a seat; one read command for seats serves that and the machine-wide view together.

## What

- **`who`**: one row per seat of the current project, `<seat>  shelf <shelf>  <session> pid <n>` for a held seat, `<seat>  shelf <shelf>  free` for a free one, `free (holder <session> gone, freed)` where the call freed it, `reserved <session>` for a reservation. Rows group by shelf.
- **`who` frees only the current project's claims**: those whose holder is gone, and reservations untaken for 10 minutes. Another project's claims are never written, with `--all` or without.
- **`who --all`**: the same rows for every other project on the machine under a heading naming the project, read-only.
- **The skill maps a user's words onto seats and shelves from `who`**, where it ran `sweep`.
- **SessionStart keeps freeing dead holders of its own project** before naming the seats to a seatless session, as before.

## Rejected

- **A read-only `seats` command in place of `sweep`.** A second seat listing beside `who`, which already lists seats.
- **Keeping `sweep` as a listing that no longer writes.** A verb named for cleaning that cleans nothing.
- **`who` staying machine-wide by default, `--all` adding free seats.** The question asked most is who holds what in this project; every other project's live sessions are the wider view, behind the flag.

## Accepted costs

- Breaking: `sweep` and its MCP tool disappear, and `who` without flags no longer lists other projects' sessions. Every script calling either changes in the same release; the skill and `docs/memory/` change with it.
- `who` writes: it frees the current project's dead claims, where it was read-only.

## Acceptance criteria

- `sweep` is unknown to the CLI and the MCP server.
- `who` lists every seat of the current project, held, free and reserved, each with its shelf, and lists no other project.
- `who` frees a current-project claim whose holder is gone and a reservation older than 10 minutes, and reports each as freed; a dead claim in another project stays as it was, with `--all` too.
- `who --all` adds every other project's seats under its name.
- The `agent-memory` skill and `docs/memory/` name `who` wherever they named `sweep`.
- Every new guard is broken and watched failing on its own test, the file restored byte-identically.
