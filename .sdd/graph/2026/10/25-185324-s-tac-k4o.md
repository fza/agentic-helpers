agentic-memory's shared-entry arrivals assume that nobody but a seated session writes a store. The arrival channels (the next prompt, the next tool call, and the idle `Stop` waiter) push each received entry's line, title included, into the receiving seat's session without being asked.

A sandboxed fdbox job can write the stores today. fdbox's job sandbox profile allows writes to all of `~/.claude` (`format-d-fdbox/.autopilot/plan/job.sb:38`), and `~/.claude/agentic-memory/` is in it. A process a job runs can append to a store's log directly, so a title of its choosing reaches an owner's session through the arrival lines.

fdbox closes that path: its boundary-audit decision closes `~/.claude/agentic-memory` to jobs, with a job's writes going through a launcher verb of fdbox's. agentic-memory changes nothing for it. The arrival lines keep their titles, and the assumption holds once fdbox's sandbox change lands.

Until then, a job can push text into seats through memory arrivals. A later sandboxed caller of agentic-memory would reopen the question, and the fix would then be for the arrival lines to carry only an entry's id and filing seat.
