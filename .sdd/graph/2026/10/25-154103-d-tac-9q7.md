`agentic-memory search` takes a repeatable `--status live|retired|superseded`, and the `memory` MCP tool `search` the key `status`, an array of those words. With it, search returns only entries in a named status. Given a status, search runs without `--term` or `--query` and lists: the most recently ended first for `retired` and `superseded` (by the time of the retire or supersede event), the most recently filed first for `live`, up to `--limit` (10 unless set, as for every search). With a term or a query beside it, the status narrows the ranked hits.

- `search --status retired` lists what ended, each line carrying its retire reason.
- `search --status retired --term 'v2\.2\.0'` finds what a release finished.
- `--status superseded` includes superseded entries as `--with-superseded` does; `--with-superseded` stays and keeps its meaning (every status, superseded included).
- Search with neither a term, a query nor a status is refused as before: `search needs --term <regex>, --query <phrase> or --status <status>`.
- A status-only listing warms nothing: it is a listing, not a match. A search with a term or a query warms its top three as before.
- Seat scope is unchanged: the own seat unless `--seat` or `--all-seats` widens it.

Why: an ended entry with its reason is the record of what finished and what was dropped, and an agent picking up work needs to read it without guessing a term that happens to match. One filter over every status serves retired entries now and live or superseded ones without another flag later.

Rejected: a `--retired` switch (covers one status, so each further one needs its own switch); a `list` subcommand (repeats search's seat, topic and limit filters in a second tool, and an agent has to choose between two ways to ask one thing).

Accepted costs: `--with-superseded` and `--status superseded` overlap, and both stay; a status-only listing ranks by time alone, never by heat or meaning.

Acceptance criteria:

1. `search --status retired` without a term or query lists only retired entries of the own seat, newest retire first, at most 10; a test asserts order, scope and limit.
2. `--status` is repeatable and combines with `--term`, `--query`, `--topic`, `--seat` and `--all-seats`; a test shows `--status retired --term` returning only retired matches.
3. `--status superseded` returns superseded entries without `--with-superseded`; `--status live` excludes retired and superseded ones.
4. A status word outside `live`, `retired`, `superseded` is refused naming the accepted words.
5. A status-only listing changes no heat; a test reads the heat before and after.
6. The MCP `search` schema carries `status` as an array of the three words, and the exact-keys rule still refuses unknown keys.
7. `README.md` and `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` name `--status` and the status-only listing.
