agentic-memory todos now hold a todolist: items kept as `item` rows in the log, addressed `<todo>#<id>`, changed through `ack`, shown in the brief and `show`, ending their todo once every item is ticked or dropped, built in agentic-helpers commit 3687169, which closes 20261028-223759-d-tac-jkj.

What was built against the decision's acceptance:
1. `new --kind todo --item` (MCP `item`) files items with distinct three-character base36 ids, named in the reply as `items <todo>: #<id> ...`; `--item` on another kind is refused.
2. `ack <todo>#<id>... --done | --undo | --decline '<why>' | --remove` and `ack <todo> --add ... | --order <id>,...`, one change a call, every address checked first; an unknown address, a position, an item on a rule, and more or fewer than one change are refused. `ack <rule id> --done | --decline` still settles a rule's pending do-now item, as before.
3. A linked item reads its target at every read: a graph target closed or settled, or a memory target retired, ticks it; a target reopening unticks it; a superseded target moves it along the chain to the newest entry; a missing one keeps it open, flagged `gone`. An explicit ack wins, and `--undo` gives the item back to its target.
4. The brief prints `<done>/<total>` on the todo's short line and up to three open items in order; `show` lists every item but removed ones (`--with-removed` adds them); JSON carries `items`, `done` and `total`.
5. A finished todolist retires the todo, `end reached: items <n>/<n>`, listed once under `ended since last brief`; a `revive` keeps it live until an item changes again.
6. Supersede and `new --absorbs` of a todo with items need `--items carry|leave`; `carry` moves every item but removed ones with ids, states and order kept, and `<old>#<id>` resolves through the supersede.
7. A todo filed with list lines in its body lands, and the reply says `<n> list lines in the text; steps go into --item`.
8. The store moves to format 5, a format-4 store migrated under the store lock with its log backed up to `backup/format-4-<stamp>/`; an older binary refuses the store.
9. agentic-helpers:agent-memory, `docs/memory/usage.md` and `docs/memory/architecture.md` describe todolists.

Two departures. A carried item whose id the successor already holds gets the new id, named `<old>#<id> is now #<new>`, rather than the successor's own item, so no address already handed out changes. A linked graph item shows the graph id, not the entry's title, since the export carries no titles.

Verified: functional tests for every criterion, each guard broken alone and caught, except two no test can reach (a drawn id never all digits; a repeated add of one id ignored). And the installed build (`go install`, then `~/go/bin/agentic-memory`) in this repository: the first call migrated the live store to format 5 with a `format-4-` backup, a todo filed with five items printed `0/5` and its first three open items in the brief, and an ack ticked one. Not yet released.
