Agentic-memory `revive` rebinds an entry retired by `end reached: session` to the reviving seat's current claim, and refuses to revive an entry whose other end condition still holds. Today the next brief silently retires such an entry again, so an agent believes it is back and loses it unannounced.

- **Session end.** Reviving an entry retired with `end reached: session` (20261029-023548-d-tac-0p5) rebinds its `until_hold` to the reviving seat's current claim (seat id and `claimed` stamp): the entry lives until that claim ends. With no seat, the revive is refused as any write is.
- **Any other end** (`changed:`, `gone:`, `merged:`, `at:`, `closed:`, `captured`, `claim`): reviving an entry retired with `end reached: <condition>` while that condition still holds is refused: `<id>: its end <condition> still holds; file it again`. Once the condition no longer holds, the revive works as before.
- **Retired or superseded by hand:** revive is unchanged.
- **Surface.** `revive` stays a CLI-only command (`revive` in `source/hooks/internal/memory/lifecycle.go`, the end check in `ends.go`). The new refusal wording goes into the `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` skill's `reference.md` and `docs/memory/` in the same change.

This narrows the revive of 20261026-215617-d-tac-45q, which undid a reached end as any retire and left it to the next brief's end check.

Why: a revive the next brief undoes without a word is worse than a refusal; the agent acts on an entry it no longer has.

Rejected: rebinding only session ends (the silent undo stays for `changed:`, `gone:`, `merged:`, `at:`, `closed:`, `captured` and `claim`); keeping revive as it is (silent loss).

Accepted cost: revive carries two paths, and the skill and docs carry one more refusal.

Acceptance (each guard broken once and watched failing):
- Reviving a session-ended entry survives the next brief and retires when the new claim ends.
- Reviving an entry whose `changed:` or `gone:` end still holds is refused with the line above.
- Reviving after the condition stopped holding works.
