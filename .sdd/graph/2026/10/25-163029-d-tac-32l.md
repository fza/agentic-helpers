The idle waiter that the `asyncRewake` `Stop` hook starts for a seated session stops as soon as there is nobody left to wake, and the turn it starts carries the arrival notice and nothing else.

- **Exit with the claim**: every poll (each 5 seconds) checks, before the log, that the seat's claim still names this session and that the session's owning `claude` process is alive. A released seat, a seat seized by `claim --force`, or an ended session makes the waiter exit 0 at that poll, without waking anything. A waiter therefore outlives its claim by at most one poll interval.
- **Seatless sessions**: the hook exits 0 at once and starts no waiter, as 20261025-162357-d-tac-427 already sets.
- **Wake text**: on an arrival the waiter exits 2 with exactly the lines `agentic-memory: new for <seat> (<n>):` and one entry line each, and nothing about any other source. Where another `asyncRewake` waiter in the same session (fdbox's `rewake.py`) also fires, each delivers its own text; agentic-memory never reads, merges or forwards another tool's deliveries.

Why: a session whose seat is released, seized or ended has no receiver for an arrival, so a waiter left running holds a process for up to 30 minutes for nothing, once per turn end. fdbox runs its own board waiter beside this one in the `idea` job, which holds seat `idea` and is woken by arrivals for it, so each waiter's text must stay its own for either tool to tell what woke the job.

Rejected: a `SessionEnd` hook killing the waiter (covers an ended session but not a released or seized seat, and needs the waiter's pid recorded); a shorter deadline (still leaves a released seat's waiter running until it lapses).

Accepted costs: each poll reads the claim file and checks one process, beside the two `stat` calls on the log.

Acceptance criteria:

1. A waiter test with an injectable poll interval releases the seat mid-wait and sees the waiter exit 0 within one interval, waking nothing; the same for a seizure by another session and for the owning process ending.
2. An arrival makes the waiter exit 2 with only the arrival lines on its output.
3. A seatless session's `Stop` hook exits 0 at once and leaves no process.
