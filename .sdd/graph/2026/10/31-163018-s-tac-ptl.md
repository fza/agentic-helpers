Shell-guard now judges commands behind zsh's precommand modifiers and shells reading a pipe from inside a compound command or through a stdin path, in agentic-helpers commit d679d97, not yet released, closing 20261031-162852-s-tac-7dt. `internal/shellwalk` reads `noglob`, `nocorrect`, `-` and `repeat N` as launchers, drops a leading `=` from a command name, hands a pipe on to every shell inside `{ }`, `if` or a loop whose statement does not redirect its own input, and reads `-`, `/dev/stdin` and a descriptor under `/dev/fd/` or `/proc/<pid>/fd/`, cleaned, as the shell's input rather than a script. BSD xargs' `-J` and `-R` take a value, so the command after them is the one xargs certainly runs. The grounding gate walks through the same code.

Every new guard was broken and its own test failed. A shell-guard row for xargs `-R` could not fail, since every word after a launcher is judged anyway, so that guard and `-J` are covered instead by rows of `TestTheWalkOffersEveryCommandItRuns` in `internal/shellwalk/shellwalk_test.go`, which assert the word xargs certainly runs. `docs/shell-guard/architecture.md` states the modifiers, the `=` reading, compound pipes and stdin operands.
