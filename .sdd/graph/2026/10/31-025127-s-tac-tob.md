agentic-memory's vector fill and agentic-tools' rebuild now fill the way sdd fills its index, in commits 0117cab and 5e0ccd9 on `main`, not yet released. Each embedding request carries at most `embed.RequestTexts` texts of whole entries or tools, has its own `embedding_timeout` deadline, and is saved as a segment as it answers. A fill takes `fill.lock` in the vector store's directory without waiting (`vectorseg.Store.TryFill`), before its snapshot; held by another process, it embeds nothing and the search scores the rows already held. README, the `embedding_timeout` option text in `plugin.json`, `docs/memory/architecture.md` and `docs/tools/architecture.md` describe both.

**Proof.** In both packages, `TestAFillFailingPartwayKeepsWhatLanded`, `TestAFillBoundsEachRequestNotTheWholeFill` and `TestAFillAnotherProcessHoldsLeavesTheEmbeddingToIt` cover saving per request, the per-request deadline and the lock. Breaking each guard (grouping, deadline, lock, in memory and tools) turned its own assertion red, and every file was restored byte-identically. End to end with the installed build, an isolated home of 40 entries of eight chunks each (320 texts) against the live embedder at `127.0.0.1:1235`, `embedding_timeout` 2s: before the change, 20 consecutive searches failed and landed nothing; after it, one search answered in 9s with all 320 texts embedded. Two searches started a second apart on a fresh store: the second answered in 1s from the rows held, embedding nothing, while the first filled the store in 9s.

**Deviation.** The tool library's rebuild runs on `reindex`, `merge` and a reach into another store, not on `find`; those paths take the lock and the per-request deadline.
