A seat's lifetime is its shelf's. A shelf is durable or temporary, and every rule of the temporary-seats directive (20261025-223045-d-tac-p2j) holds with shelf in place of seat.

Why: several seats share one shelf; a lifetime per seat would purge each agent's filings as that agent's clock ran out, while the others still work from the same memory.

## What

- **The clock starts when the last seat on the shelf closes.** A claim of any seat on the shelf before retention runs out stops it.
- **The purge removes the shelf's entries and every seat on the shelf.** Entries filed `--for` seats on other shelves survive, as in the temporary-seats directive.
- **`lifetime` and `claim --lifetime` act on the shelf of the seat named.** Any seat on a shelf may change its lifetime. The setting is the shelf's, no longer a key of the seat's `seats.json` entry. A reservation (20261025-223640-d-tac-d2p) counts as a seat in use: the clock does not run while any seat on the shelf is held or reserved.
- **`agentic-memory drop <shelf>` purges a temporary shelf at once**, from any process, seat or not: the same purge as a run-out retention, reported as `dropped <shelf> (<n> seats, <m> entries)`. Refused, changing nothing, while any seat on the shelf is held or reserved (`<seat> holds a seat on <shelf>`), and for a durable shelf (`<shelf> is durable; make it temporary first`). MCP: tool `drop` with key `shelf`.
- **The `agent-memory` skill maps lifetime words onto `--lifetime`** without asking: "temporary" → `temporary`, a span ("2 days", "for an hour") → that duration, "durable" or "keep it" → `durable`, combined with any seat or shelf words ("main, shared, temporary").

## Rejected

- **A lifetime on both seat and shelf, two clocks.** Most control, but two settings and a rule for every pairing of a temporary seat with a durable shelf and the reverse.
- **The lifetime staying on the seat.** A shared shelf would lose each agent's notes as that agent's clock ran out, while the others still work on the row.

- **`lifetime <value> --shelf <name>` from any process.** Ends a row with a mistyped duration as easily as on purpose, and gives every process the power to shorten any shelf's lifetime.
- **Ending a shelf only from a seat on it.** A row with no live agent could not be ended; it would wait out its retention.

## Accepted costs

- One seat on a shared shelf can change the lifetime for every seat on it.
- Any process able to run agentic-memory can drop any temporary shelf of the project no seat is using.
- A row with a live agent is dropped only after that agent closes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three seats on a temporary shelf: the first two closing record no close time; the last closing starts the clock; a claim of any of them before retention runs out stops it.
- Once retention runs out, the purge removes the shelf's entries, except those filed `--for` seats on other shelves, and every seat on the shelf; the shelf name and each seat name can be claimed anew.
- `lifetime <value>` from any seat on the shelf changes the shelf's lifetime, and every seat on it reads the new value.
- A held or reserved seat on the shelf keeps the clock from starting.
- `drop <shelf>` on a temporary shelf no seat uses purges it as a run-out retention would and reports the counts; with a seat held or reserved on it, or on a durable shelf, it is refused and changes nothing.
- `docs/memory/` and the `agent-memory` skill describe the shelf's lifetime in the same change.
