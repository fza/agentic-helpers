A session asked for a seat in loose words or another language claims it and answers with where work stands, in agentic-helpers commits dcc8df3 and 3ae9d50, not yet released, closing 20261030-180319-d-tac-wvo.

**Built.** The `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` skill's quiet section names the exception for a claim the user requests, and its seat section says what the overview holds: the state slot, what is due, open todos with their next items, decisions waiting on the user, in the user's language, outcomes only. `docs/memory/usage.md` says the same.

**Deviation: the seatless notice carries the trigger.** The skill alone did not reach a fresh session: told only `Haupt-Session`, a headless session in a scratch project with a free seat `main` never loaded the memory skill and answered that the prompt gave it no task. The notice a session holding no seat gets at its start (`internal/memory/hooks.go`, `seatless`) now ends: `A prompt naming a seat in other words or another language ("main seat", "Haupt-Session") asks to claim it: claim it, then answer with where work stands (agentic-helpers:agent-memory)`. `TestSessionStart` asserts the sentence and failed with it removed; `docs/plugin/usage.md` and `docs/memory/usage.md` describe it. The decision had rejected a prompt hook detecting claim requests; this is no detection, one sentence more in an existing start notice.

**Proven live** on the installed build: the same headless session told `Haupt-Session` claimed seat `main` and answered with its state, its due todo and the release waiting on the owner.

**Deviation: language.** The overview came in English. A bare `Haupt-Session` is too little text for the agent to take German as the user's language; acceptance 3's German answer was not met. A longer German prompt was not tried.

The live run also surfaced the headless hang of 20261030-181944-s-tac-ueg.
