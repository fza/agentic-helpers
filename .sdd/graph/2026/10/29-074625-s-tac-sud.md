`agentic-memory who` and `who --all` leave out free seats that filed nothing and had nothing used in 14 days and end each project's block with a count line naming the flag that lists them, in agentic-helpers commit fbabdf4 on main, not yet released, completing 20261029-023244-d-tac-cjx.

Every acceptance criterion is asserted by TestWhoCountsUnusedFreeSeatsRatherThanListingThem in `source/hooks/internal/memory/who_test.go`: an unused free seat is absent from `who` and `who --all` and counted in the line; `--unused` lists it; a held seat with nothing filed still shows; `who --json` and the MCP `who` tool list every seat. The `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` skill and `docs/memory/usage.md` change in the same commit.

One deviation in wording: under `who --all` the count line names `agentic-memory who --all --unused`, the command that actually lists those seats there, where the directive spelled only `agentic-memory who --unused`; the project's own `who` keeps `who --unused`.

Live on the installed binary: `agentic-memory who --all` dropped from 121 to 57 lines, ending `+55 unused seats: agentic-memory who --all --unused`.
