The agentic-memory brief prints its space-left line only while a store has under 10% of its limit left, and only that store's part, while every `attach`, `add` and `update` reply still ends with its store's figure. This narrows the space-shown rule of 20261029-024444-d-tac-x9j, which printed the line in every brief.

- **Brief.** `space left: attachments <left> of <limit>, tools <left> of <limit>` prints only for a store with under 10% of its limit left: one store low prints its part alone, both low print both, neither prints no line. The brief runs at claim, resume and compaction.
- **Write replies** unchanged: every `attach` reply ends with the attachment figure, every `add` and `update` reply with the tools figure.
- **Unchanged from x9j:** `attachments_total_mb` (default 2048, overridable), `tools_total_mb` (default 500, `config.yaml` only), the fixed 1 MiB per-tool cap, pruning in the SessionStart purge pass and inside a write needing room, space computed from sizes recorded at write time, and the accepted drift between recorded totals and the disk.

Why: the hidden-message rule of 20261027-222117-d-tac-yjy keeps a message to the moments it changes what the agent does. The figure matters when the agent writes and when space runs low; with plenty left, the line costs about 60 characters at every claim and compaction for nothing.

Rejected: the line in every brief, as x9j set it; the figure in write replies only (no warning before a write is refused).

Accepted cost: an agent planning a large attach sees the figure only in the reply, unless space is low.

Acceptance (each guard broken once and watched failing):
- A brief with both stores above 10% left has no space line.
- A brief with one store under 10% left prints that store's part only.
- Every `attach`, `add` and `update` reply carries its store's figure.
- `agentic-helpers:agent-memory`, `agentic-helpers:agent-tools` and `docs/` describe when the brief prints the line.
