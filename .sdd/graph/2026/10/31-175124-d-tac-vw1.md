Scratch folders move to `.tmp/seats/`, and work an agent keeps beyond its scratch goes to `.tmp/keep/<name>/`, so no folder an agent names can take a later seat's name. Under the layout of 20261030-082426-d-tac-h68, a seat's folder is `.tmp/claude/<seat>/` while the agent-scratch skill and `docs/scratch/usage.md` invite keeps at `.tmp/claude/<name>/`: agents create `.tmp/claude/probes/` and the like, and claiming a seat named `probes` later finds an unmarked folder, prints `(a keep, never swept)`, and mixes the seat's throwaway into somebody's keep for good.

**Layout.** A seat's folder is `.tmp/seats/<seat>/`, marked with `.agentic-scratch` as before; a session holding no seat writes under `.tmp/seats/<session-id>/`. `.tmp/seats/` belongs to agentic-helpers alone: an agent never creates anything there itself. Work a later session reads goes to `.tmp/keep/<name>/`, never swept, beside `.tmp/drafts/` and the project's own files at the root of `.tmp/`, which stay untouched. The claim and post-compaction line becomes `scratch: .tmp/seats/<seat>/`, and every skill quoting it changes with it. Sweep rules, `scratch_sweep`, the marker and the 7-day reap stay as 20261030-082426-d-tac-h68 set them, now applied under `.tmp/seats/`.

**Migration, by moving, never deleting.** At session start, each marked `.tmp/claude/<seat>/` moves to `.tmp/seats/<seat>/` and each `.tmp/claude/<session-id>/` to `.tmp/seats/<session-id>/`. Every other entry of `.tmp/claude/`, an unmarked folder or a loose file, moves to `.tmp/keep/<name>` once nothing in it changed for 24 hours, a taken name getting `-2`, `-3` and so on; an empty `.tmp/claude/` then goes. The same rule keeps `.tmp/seats/` clean: an unmarked entry there that is not named for a session moves to `.tmp/keep/` after 24 quiet hours. A claim finding an unmarked folder at its seat's name moves it to `.tmp/keep/<seat>/` at once and then makes the seat's folder, so the claim never prints `(a keep, never swept)` again. Each move is said in one line naming where it went.

**Rejected.** Keeping seat folders at `.tmp/claude/` with keeps moved to `.tmp/keep/`: `.tmp/claude/` reads as the agent's own room, and it would keep inviting folders there. Keeps at plain `.tmp/<name>/`: they mix with the project's own files and a move could collide with one of its names. Moving strays only at claim time: names nobody claims would clutter the scratch root for good. Moving strays at once: a session still writing there under the old skill would lose its paths mid-work, hence the 24-hour quiet period.

**Accepted costs.** Existing keeps change path, so notes or scripts naming `.tmp/claude/<name>/` break. The claim line changes, a wording contract skills quote. One directory walk of `.tmp/claude/` and `.tmp/seats/` per session start.

**Acceptance.**
- A claim makes `.tmp/seats/<seat>/` with its marker and prints `scratch: .tmp/seats/<seat>/`; a seatless session writes under `.tmp/seats/<session-id>/`.
- A marked `.tmp/claude/<seat>/` and a `.tmp/claude/<session-id>/` land under `.tmp/seats/` at session start, contents intact.
- An unmarked folder or loose file in `.tmp/claude/`, or an unmarked non-session entry in `.tmp/seats/`, moves to `.tmp/keep/` only after 24 quiet hours, a taken name suffixed; nothing is deleted but an empty `.tmp/claude/`.
- A claim hitting an unmarked folder at its seat's name moves it to `.tmp/keep/<seat>/` and makes a marked seat folder.
- Release, session end and the 7-day reap act under `.tmp/seats/` exactly as before; rename and handover carry the folder there.
- The agent-scratch and agent-memory skills, `docs/scratch/`, and every place quoting the claim line describe the new layout, `.tmp/keep/` and the migration.
