An agentic-memory seat can be marked private through `agentic-memory claim <seat> --private`, the MCP `claim` key `private: true`, or the CLI-only `agentic-memory private on|off`, which hides it from every other project's `who --all` while its own project lists it ending `private`, in agentic-helpers commit 0e24154 on main, not yet released, completing 20261029-023424-d-tac-z7t.

Each criterion with its test in `source/hooks/internal/memory/private_test.go`:

1. `claim --private` hides the seat from another project's `who --all` and `--json`, its own project shows it with `private`, `private off` shows it again, and the mark survives a release and a later claim: TestAPrivateSeatLeavesNoTraceInAnotherProjectsWho.
2. An MCP call naming `private` is refused with the CLI line, and an MCP `claim` with `private: true` marks the seat: TestPrivateIsNoMCPToolButAnMCPClaimMarksTheSeat.
3. A launcher's `claim --session ... --private` marks the seat it claims for another session: TestALauncherClaimForAnotherSessionMarksTheSeatPrivate.
4. `private` without a held seat or without on or off is refused: TestPrivateIsRefusedWithoutASeatOrAnOnOff.

The `agentic-helpers:agent-memory` skill (`SKILL.md` and `reference.md`) and `docs/memory/` change in the same commit.

Live on the installed binary: `agentic-memory private on` printed `main is private: no other project's who lists it`, the project's own `who` line for the seat ended `private`, and `private off` printed `main is no longer private: other projects' who --all lists it`.
