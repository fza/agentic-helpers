Done for 20261025-182429-d-tac-fhx: commit 3a61e5a, released as v2.4.0 (release commit 2973181).

- The ledger's evidence carries `graph`, the `graphState()` the reads were recorded under. Bash and MCP crediting, draft grounding and the gate each hold the evidence against the current state first, and a differing state clears query, term and listings; grounded drafts keep their own state. SessionStart with `source: compact` forgets the session's reads and keeps its grounded drafts. The `turn` mode, its UserPromptSubmit wiring in `plugin/hooks/hooks.json` and the `<task-notification>` carve-out are gone.
- Wording: the reads lines say `since the graph last changed` and `Standing reads:`, in place of `ran this turn` and `This turn carries:`; no skill quoted the old text.
- Tests: `credit_test.go` (credit standing while the graph stands, a read after a change clearing only older credit, only a compaction forgetting the reads, a draft saved after the graph moved not grounded, the `turn` mode refused and unwired); the earlier per-turn tests now compact instead. Each `against` call (gate, Bash record, MCP record, draft record), the compaction branch, the `compact`-only condition and draft keeping on forget were each broken and caught.
- Docs: `docs/grounding-gate/usage.md` ("How long the reads count"), `docs/grounding-gate/architecture.md`, `docs/plugin/architecture.md`, the package comment and `agentic-helpers:agent-grounding` in the same commit.
