agentic-memory's store lock does not wait out an interrupted lock call. Observed, by reading the code: the store lock of the store the agentic-memory directive built (20261025-014326-d-tac-mvn) calls `syscall.Flock(..., LOCK_EX)` once in `memory/seatkeys.go:74` and returns the error, so a signal arriving while it waits fails the call with `EINTR` as `taking the store lock: interrupted system call`.

Expected: a blocking lock call retries on `EINTR` and keeps waiting, as the log lock in the same store does (`memorylog/lock_unix.go:14`).

The deviation: a hook or command interrupted while another holds the store lock fails where it should have waited. Confidence is medium on the cost side only: how often a signal lands during that wait has not been measured.
