Every agentic-helpers option holding a length of time takes a span, such as `90d`, `2m` or `120s`, and its key names no unit, so one spelling serves every time option and matches sdd's (`embedding.timeout: 2m`).

Why: time options came in three forms. `retired_body_days` takes whole days, the planned `tools_version_days` of 20261030-000535-d-tac-0y1 would too, `temporary_seat_lifetime` and `orphan_store_grace` take spans, `embedding_timeout` of 20261030-083307-d-tac-q74 takes a span as sdd does, and `shell_guard_ask_timeout` takes seconds. Nothing in a key said which form its value needs, and a value in the wrong form fell back to the default.

**Grammar.** One span reader in `internal/pluginconfig` replaces the two copies in `internal/memory/lifetime.go` and `internal/home/grace.go`: `0`, or a whole count followed by `s`, `m`, `h`, `d` or `w`, or a Go duration such as sdd prints (`1m0s`). `agentic-memory lifetime` and every time option read through it.

**Keys.** `retired_body_days` becomes `retired_body_lifetime` (default `90d`, at least `14d`); the planned `tools_version_days` is built as `tools_version_lifetime` (default `90d`, at least `14d`); `shell_guard_ask_timeout` keeps its name and takes a span (default `2m`, at most `1h`, `0` waits without limit). `temporary_seat_lifetime`, `orphan_store_grace` and `embedding_timeout` stay as they are. `shell_guard_ask_timeout` becomes a string option in `userConfig`, dropping the 0 to 3600 dialog bound 20261030-083534-d-tac-ws7 gave it; the option reader clamps the span to at most `1h` instead.

**Old forms.** For one release `retired_body_days` is still read, its number as days, where `retired_body_lifetime` is unset, and `agentic-helpers check` names it at session start: `agentic-helpers: retired_body_days is now retired_body_lifetime (90d); rename it in config.yaml`. A bare number in `shell_guard_ask_timeout` keeps meaning seconds, since existing answers in Claude Code's settings hold one.

**Rejected.** Whole numbers with the unit in the key name (`_days`, `_seconds`): the dialog could bound them, but three keys would be renamed and `embedding_timeout` would stop matching sdd. Leaving each key in its own form: three spellings stay, and a wrong form keeps failing only as a check line.

**Accepted costs.** One renamed key read under its old name for a release. `shell_guard_ask_timeout` loses its dialog bound. A span reader accepting both the short and the Go form.

**Acceptance.**
- A table-driven test reads every span form, `0`, and Go durations through the one reader, and refuses anything else.
- Every time option resolves through it; `internal/memory` and `internal/home` keep no span grammar of their own.
- `retired_body_days: 90` alone gives a 90-day retention and the check line; with `retired_body_lifetime` also set, the new key wins.
- `shell_guard_ask_timeout` reads `120` as two minutes and `2m` the same; values beyond `1h` clamp.
- README `Options`, `docs/plugin/usage.md` and the skills naming these keys use the new names and spans.
