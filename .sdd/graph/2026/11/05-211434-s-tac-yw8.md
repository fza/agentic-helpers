A seat's state ledger now keeps itself small, built in commits 002d9192, b057a737 and 76a6b220 on branch `v2`, not yet released; it closes the directive 20261104-130942-d-tac-c7y, whose every acceptance criterion holds.

- `ack state --add` without `--until` is refused, `ack state --add needs --until:` naming the forms it takes, and `--until none` adds a line only a tick or a remove ends (002d9192, `feat: refuse a state line added without --until, taking --until none for a line only a tick ends`).
- Every hook line ends at the seat's next claim, `the seat was claimed since`; a claim the session holds already, taken again, is no next claim (b057a737, `feat: end the state ledger's hook lines at the seat's next claim`).
- The count nudge is replaced by marks calibrated per seat from the seat's own history, kept in the slot `ledger-pressure/<seat id>`: above the high mark, the `ack state --add` reply (with its backoff) and the brief list the five oldest open lines, each cut to 12 words, with the call that ticks each (76a6b220, `feat: replace the ledger count nudge with self-calibrating marks listing the oldest open lines`). A release that does not know the slot keeps it as a key of an unknown purpose.

Acceptance, criterion by criterion: the refused add and `--until none`, hook lines from before a claim ending after it, the marks from a fixed seat history with the cold-start borrowing and the 10/6 default, and the 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 backoff with its reset at the low mark are each shown by tests in `source/hooks/internal/memory`, not in a live session; the agent-memory skill, `docs/memory/usage.md` and `docs/memory/architecture.md` describe the rules.
