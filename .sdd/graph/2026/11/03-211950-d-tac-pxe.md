agentic-memory's landed scan judges a todo's open items by ranking them within their todo against the whole commit, while entries and state lines keep the subject test at a cosine of 0.75 (`landedSimilarity`), and an embedder outage keeps the commits it left unjudged for the next round. Both change the subject test of the landed-commit detection (20261101-110914-d-tac-pu1), which compared every target with the commit subject at one threshold.

**Items ranked within their todo.** For an open item, the embedded text is the whole commit: its message, subject and body, and its changed file names, cut to 2,000 runes. Per landing and todo, the item the landing sits closest to matches where its cosine is at least `landedItemFloor` (0.55) and at least `landedItemMargin` (0.05) ahead of the todo's next closest item; a todo with one item judged needs the floor alone. The match strength is the cosine stretched onto 0..1 above the floor. A near tie flags nothing, and the items of two todos never rank against each other. Both constants live in `internal/memory/landed.go` and are tuned to embeddinggemma-300m. Why: an item never reaches 0.75 against the commit that does it (0.56 to 0.67 measured under that model), while a todo's items crowd each other within a few hundredths, so an absolute threshold either flags nothing or flags every sibling. A hint naming the wrong item costs more than a missed one, which the margin buys.

**An embedder outage keeps its commits.** An embedder that fails or times out leaves the checkout's scan mark before the first commit whose texts it left unjudged, so the next round reads that commit again rather than never judging it. No embedder configured (`embed.ErrNotConfigured`) still skips the subject test alone, and the branch and path tests run as before.

Rejected:

- Comparing items with the subject alone: a subject names the change, while the body and the file names name the work an item describes.
- Binding an item to a commit when work on it starts: one more call for the agent at every start, and a bind forgotten matches nothing.
- A nudge after each commit asking which item it settled: hidden hook noise on every commit, against keeping hidden messages to one per cause and only when they change what the agent does (20261027-222117-d-tac-yjy).

Accepted costs: both constants are tuned to one embedding model and need re-measuring if the store's model changes; a commit doing two items of one todo flags at most the closer one; while an outage lasts, each round re-reads up to a batch of commits from the mark.

Settled: built on `main` (commits f653c4d6 and d123edf7), the ranking table-tested in `internal/memory/landed_internal_test.go` and the outage in `landedrounds_test.go`, and described in `docs/memory/architecture.md` and `docs/memory/usage.md`; nothing follows from this entry.
