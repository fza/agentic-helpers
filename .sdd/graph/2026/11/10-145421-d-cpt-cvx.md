The graph keeps no overview of agentic-helpers' pieces and their layout: `AGENTS.md`, `README.md` and `docs/` carry it, and the rule that a landing change updates the `docs/` pages it makes untrue keeps them current with each piece, while a graph overview changes only when someone thinks to supersede it. Graph entries keep the why of each piece.

The overview fact 20261025-195434-s-cpt-ihb is closed by this decision, not superseded. It went stale without anyone noticing: it still names `agentic-capture` and the grounding gate as pieces here, both gone since v2.29.0, omits shell-guard, the session record, the credential scanner and the `agentic-helpers` command, and gives storage paths such as `~/.claude/agentic-memory/<project>/` that the data root under `$XDG_DATA_HOME/agentic-helpers` replaced. The per-piece docs layout (20261025-165536-d-tac-9in) already made `docs/` the place describing what each piece does, and `AGENTS.md` states that `.sdd/` answers why the hooks are the way they are and `docs/` what they do.

## Rejected

- **Superseding it with a current overview fact:** a second copy of what the docs say, which goes stale again with the next new or moved piece, as this one did.

## Accepted costs

- A reader of the graph without the checkout finds no overview of the pieces in it.

Settled: closing the fact is the whole of it; nothing is built or changed in the tree.
