A temporary seat's own slot keys (`traps`, `do-now`, `skipped`, `brief`, `seen`, `ledger-ended`) stay until its shelf expires and the registry drops the seat, and are not cleared when the seat's session ends.

Why: the SessionStart housekeeping already drops every seat key whose seat the registry no longer holds (20261102-225603-s-tac-inq), and a temporary shelf is purged once its retention runs out after its last seat closes (`temporary_seat_lifetime`, one day by default). A store running a temporary seat per job therefore carries at most about a retention's worth of jobs' seat keys, six small keys each. The large key counts measured in fdbox (2,863 `ladder` keys, about 2,500 seats' keys) predate the session-end clearing of commit 00dff2a, which removes the per-session share.

Rejected: clearing a temporary seat's keys at its session end. It saves about a day of small keys, but a seat claimed again within its retention would lose its seen marks, so its brief reprints every entry in full at a token cost, and its trap index is rebuilt. Deferring the choice until key counts are measured after the next release was rejected too: the bound above holds by construction.

Nothing follows: the existing reclaim and purge already bound these keys.
