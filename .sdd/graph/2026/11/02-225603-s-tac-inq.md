agentic-memory's `sessions.slots` now sheds the keys nothing reads again and shrinks once mostly empty, built in agentic-helpers commit 00dff2a on `main`, not yet released, closing the slot-file growth gap (20261102-225503-s-tac-zbd).

A SessionEnd clears the session's `ladder`, `waiter`, `graph-seen` and `fork` keys beside its `shown` and `instructions` keys (`clearEnded` in `internal/memory/sessionslots.go`). The SessionStart pass drops every seat key (`traps`, `do-now`, `skipped`, `brief`, `seen`, `ledger-ended`) whose seat the registry no longer holds or holds expired, and an unreadable registry drops none. `slots.Reclaim` writes the file anew at the size its kept keys need once a quarter of its pairs or fewer stay in use. `docs/memory/architecture.md` describes the keys and the shrink.

Tests: `TestASessionEndClearsTheStateNothingReadsAfterIt`, `TestTheStateOfASeatGoneFromTheRegistryIsReclaimed` and `TestAnUnreadableRegistryReclaimsNoSeatsState` in `internal/memory/slotgrowth_test.go`, `TestAReclaimLeavingFewKeysShrinksTheFile` and `TestAnEmptyFileReclaimedKeepsItsFirstSize` in `internal/slots/slots_test.go`.

Live, on the store that measured 16,216 keys in an 8 GiB file, the reclaim kept 9,748 keys of seats and sessions still held, and the shrink brought the file to 512 MiB nominal, about 128 MB on disk.
