agentic-helpers drops every pointer to the sdd-helpers plugin, which is retired: sdd, the `fza/sdd` fork from 0.18.0-alpha.3.fza.43 on, runs the grounding gate and graph capture itself, in its capture procedure, so nothing is left for agentic-helpers to name. This reverses the notice, the kept prefix and the graph dependency of the move to sdd-helpers (20261107-100211-d-tac-d1x).

## What changes

- **Removed:** the SessionStart line of `agentic-helpers check` naming sdd-helpers, with `internal/pieces/moved.go`, its test, the fixtures under `test/fixtures/installedplugins/` and the `InstalledPlugins` field of `pieces.Env`; the "Moved to sdd-helpers" sections of `README.md`, `plugin/README.md` and `docs/plugin/usage.md`.
- **Removed:** `grounding gate` from the hook-line openings `hookio.Neutralize` quotes (20261105-211638-s-tac-3fd). No hook in a session prints a `GROUNDING GATE:` line any more, sdd included, so a filed line opening with it impersonates nothing.
- **Kept:** `docs/plugin/usage.md` says agentic-helpers carries no grounding gate and no capture, and that a `config.yaml` naming `grounding_gate_enabled`, `capture_enabled` or `capture_remove_draft` gets the line for an unknown key; `AGENTS.md` and `docs/index.md` say sdd runs both.
- **Graph:** `github.com/fza/sdd-helpers` leaves this graph's dependencies, through `sdd repo remove`; no entry here cites an sdd-helpers entry by id.

## Rejected

- **Rewording the line to point at the `/sdd` skill and `start_session`:** it would print in every graph project at every session start, also where the skill is installed, with no way to turn it off, the cost the move to sdd-helpers already rejected for an unconditional line.
- **Repointing it at the `fza/sdd` fork:** sdd's own setup (`sdd init` and its skill) introduces sdd; agentic-helpers has no part of it left to announce.

## Accepted costs

- A user still running sdd-helpers gets no word from agentic-helpers; telling them is the final sdd-helpers release's job.

## Acceptance

1. No tracked file outside `.sdd/` names sdd-helpers; `go vet ./...` and `go test -race ./...` pass.
2. A filed line opening `GROUNDING GATE:` prints unquoted in hook context, and the test asserting it fails while the prefix is still listed.
3. `.sdd/config.yaml` declares no dependency on `github.com/fza/sdd-helpers`.
