A seat's state ledger keeps itself small: every new line names when it ends, hook lines end at the next claim, and a nudge whose marks calibrate per seat from the seat's own history lists the oldest open lines with the call that ticks each. A seat's ledger had grown to about 60 open lines, 55 of them settled: lines were added like a log (each step a new line, the one it replaced never ticked), only one carried an end condition, so captures, merges and closes ended nothing, and the nudge "ledger holds N lines; drop the settled ones" named a count, no candidates, and was passed over against the task at hand. Open work that lived only in state lines went unseen. Every project's agents run the same code and meet the same pull.

**What changes.** This builds on the seat state ledger (20261101-190255-d-tac-6hl), where a line with neither ids nor `--until` lived until the agent dropped it and the `ack` reply named a count past 12 open lines, and the hook line ends of 20261103-164659-d-tac-eaa.
- `agentic-memory ack state --add` refuses a line without `--until` (any of its existing forms) or an explicit `--until none`, accepted on `ack state --add` only; the refusal names the forms.
- Hook lines (`landed`, `captured`, `dirty`, `asked`) end at the seat's next claim, beside the newest-12-and-a-day end of 20261103-164659-d-tac-eaa; a session claiming a seat it already holds is no new claim.
- The nudge, in the reply to an add and in the brief, lists up to 5 of the seat's oldest own open lines, each with its tick call, instead of a count.

**Self-calibrating marks.** Fixed marks would encode one machine's way of working; other usage differs. Each ledger change records time, the seat's own open-line count and whether a line was added or settled (ticked, dropped, ended), kept in a slot of the seat's store (14 days, at most 256 events), so every project calibrates on its own and continuously.
- Low mark: the median open count at settle events, the size the seat holds while work closes.
- High mark: low plus the seat's spread, p75 minus p25 of the open count over the window, at least 2.
- The nudge fires on the first add above the high mark, then only on the 2nd, 4th, 8th and 16th further add, then every 16th. A settle while the count stays above the low mark leaves the backoff where it is; once the count falls to the low mark, the backoff resets. The brief lists the same lines whenever the count is above the high mark, with no backoff.
- A ledger that keeps growing while nothing settles fills the window with higher counts, so both marks rise and the pressure eases; settled work pulls the median down again and the next growth nudges at full strength.
- Cold start: a seat with fewer than 10 settle events takes the median of the project's other seats' low marks and of their high marks, else 10 high and 6 low.

**Rejected.** Fixed marks, measured once on one laptop: other usage differs. A moving average with high = 1.5 times low: one burst drags it for days, and the factor is again fixed. A hard cap refusing adds: blocks work and invites blind ticking. Listing similar lines on add for the new line to replace: similarity guesses wrong and lines without a successor still never end.

**Accepted costs.** One more flag per added line and a retry where it is missing; landed hints last one session; a slot of up to 256 events per seat and a sort of at most 256 numbers per add.

**Acceptance.**
- An add without `--until` is refused with the forms named; `--until none` is accepted.
- Hook lines from before a claim are gone after it.
- With a seat history fixed in a test, the marks come out as the median and spread defined above; cold start borrows the project's seats, else 10/6.
- The nudge fires above the high mark, backs off 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 while nothing settles, and fires again after the count fell to the low mark and rose above the high mark.
- The agent-memory skill, `docs/memory/usage.md` and `docs/memory/architecture.md` describe it.
