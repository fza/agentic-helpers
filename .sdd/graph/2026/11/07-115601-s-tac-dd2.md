`agentic-memory show state` and MCP `show` with the id `state` print only the live ledger lines and fold the rest behind `--with-history` and `--all-items`, and a `landed` or `captured` hook line ends on its own once it is out of the newest 12 open ones and over a day old; built in commit 922d480 (`feat: fold show state to live lines and end hook lines out of the newest 12 after a day`), closing 20261103-164659-d-tac-eaa. Every commit named here, the deviations included, first shipped in release v2.28.0.

**Proof**, criterion by criterion, each shown by a test in `source/hooks/internal/memory` that passes under `go test -race` at v2.29.0:

1. Default output: `stateLines` (`ledger.go`) prints every live agent line, the open `dirty` and `asked` lines and the newest 12 open `landed` and `captured` lines, counting the older hook lines into one fold and every other ticked or ended line into the other; `showState` ends on `(<n> older landed and captured: agentic-memory show state --with-history)` and `(<n> settled: agentic-memory show state --all-items)`. Test: `TestShowStatePrintsWhatIsLive` (`showstate_test.go`).
2. Flags: `--with-history` adds every `landed` and `captured` line, `--all-items` every other settled line, and each fold count is exactly what its flag adds. MCP `show` takes `with_history` beside `all_items` (`commands.go`), and its JSON for `state` carries the same lines as the text, with `folded.history` and `folded.settled` (`stateJSON`, `jsonFolded` in `ledger.go`). Test: `TestShowStateFlagsAddTheirGroup`, which compares the text's line ids with the JSON's for each flag combination.
3. Hook line end: `viewLine` (`ledger.go`) reads a `landed` or `captured` line as ended, reason `out of the newest 12`, once at least `briefHookLines` (12) newer hook lines stand open by the logged state and it is older than `hookLineLifetime` (a day), worked out at each read and never written. Tests: `TestALandedLineEndsOutOfTheNewestTwelveOnceADayOld` (under a day stays open, over a day ends, a ticked newer line keeps it open, the log unchanged by the read) and `TestTheAskedLineNeverEndsByAge`.
4. Nudge: `ledger.unsettled` reads each line's logged state, never the worked-out end, so a line that ends on its own keeps the nudge unanswered until the agent ticks, removes or cites it (commit 1599e82, `feat: a memory nudge stays unanswered until landed and captured lines since it settle`). Tests: `TestALineEndingOnItsOwnNeverAnswersANudge` and `TestAReminderWaitsForTheLandedLinesSinceIt` (`reminderanswer_test.go`).
5. Brief: `stateSection` lists the newest 12 open hook lines and folds the other open ones into `(<n> more: agentic-memory show state --with-history)`, a count of open lines only, and `endedLines` never names a hook line under `ended since last brief`. Tests: `TestTheBriefFoldCountsOpenHookLinesOnly` and `TestTheBriefFoldsHookLinesPastTheNewestTwelve` (`ledger_test.go`).

`docs/memory/usage.md`, `docs/memory/architecture.md` and the agent-memory skill's reference describe the folds, the flags, the JSON counts and both hook line ends.

**Deviations:**

- Every hook line, `landed`, `captured`, `dirty` and `asked` alike, also ends at the seat's next claim, reason `the seat was claimed since`, so the newest-12-and-a-day end only reaches a seat held for more than a day (commit 8c88115, `feat: end the state ledger's hook lines at the seat's next claim`, directed by 20261104-130942-d-tac-c7y and recorded built in 20261105-211434-s-tac-yw8). Test: `TestHookLinesEndAtTheSeatsNextClaim`.
- The nudge waits on every unsettled `landed` and `captured` line added since the seat's claim, not only since the nudge, and names them (commit 1df452d, `fix: name every open landed and captured line in the reminder and the ledger nudge`); a line from an earlier claim never holds it. Tests: `TestWorkLandedBeforeTheReminderKeepsItOwed` and `TestAHookLineFromAnEarlierClaimLeavesTheReminderAlone` (`hooklinesnamed_test.go`).
- In the brief, a `landed` line whose commit a landed round matched to an open agent line is shown nested under that line instead of among the hook lines; `show state` prints it in both places.
