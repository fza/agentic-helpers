Landed-commit detection for agentic-memory is built on `main` in commit 984ba7d (merged as 483ee30, pushed, not released) and proven live: a seated session that filed a todo linked to 20261101-111421-d-tac-4ts received, at the tool call right after the merge commit 4b9e3469 naming that id, the hidden line `m-aw94c1 by 4b9e3469 "chore: merge shell-guard …" (id)` with its `retire` command. It closes the detection directive 20261101-110914-d-tac-pu1 and its refinements 20261101-111115-d-tac-js0 (linked ids), 20261101-112148-d-tac-6jm (incremental asynchronous rounds), 20261101-112431-d-tac-9t4 (merge evidence) and 20261101-113129-d-tac-tga (score ranking).

What landed: the scan in `source/hooks/internal/memory/landed.go`; a due hook starts a detached `agentic-memory landed-round <token>` and returns, one round per checkout under a lock file in the cache, a dead round's lock taken over; plugin option `landed_scan_interval` (default 10m, floor 1m) in `pluginconfig`, `plugin.json`, the plugin README and `docs/memory/usage.md`; the brief flag `retire? landed: <sha> <subject>` and the prompt and tool-call line; `merged:` ends met after branch deletion through the recorded tip or the default `Merge branch '<branch>'` commit's second parent; the filing hint for a todo without `--until` or `--item`; the skill rule that a todo or trap is a lead, checked against the repository before open work is presented. Every acceptance bullet of the five directives has a named test in `landed_test.go`, `landedrounds_test.go` and the `landed*_internal_test.go` files; every new guard was broken and caught by its own test.

Deviations:
- One mapping per entry or item: the ranking of 20261101-113129-d-tac-tga keeps the highest-scoring commit, which overrides the several-mappings-per-entry bullet of 20261101-110914-d-tac-pu1.
- An item's brief flag reads `ack #<item>? landed: <sha> <subject>`, since an item is ticked rather than retired.
- The similarity threshold, which the directives leave unset, is the constant 0.75 in code, pinned by tests; it is not a plugin option, keeping the option surface to the scan interval.
- The detached start itself has no test, as it needs a real process; the new `process.Detached` helper repeats the detached start the store-format migration already carries, and the two are left unmerged.

Noticed in the doing: a brief folding an already-seen entry into `(seen)` dropped its landed flag; fixed in the same commit and covered by `TestASeenEntryStillCarriesItsLandedFlag`. A mapping is dropped the moment its line is delivered, so a hint delivered just before a compaction is lost from the post-compaction brief; that is the build as specified, and changing it is its own decision.
