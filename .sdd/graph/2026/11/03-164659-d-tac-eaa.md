`agentic-memory show state` and MCP `show` with the id `state` print what is live in the ledger and fold the rest, and a `landed` or `captured` hook line ends on its own once it is out of the newest 12 and a day old. This replaces two rules of the seat state ledger decision (20261101-190255-d-tac-6hl): that `show state` prints the whole ledger, and that hook lines stay open until the agent settles them.

- **Default output:** the old slot where it waits, every open agent line whole with the commits nested under it, the open `dirty` and `asked` lines, the newest 12 open `landed` and `captured` lines, then one fold line per hidden group, each naming the flag that prints it: `(<n> older landed and captured: agentic-memory show state --with-history)` and `(<n> settled: agentic-memory show state --all-items)`.
- **Flags:** `--with-history` adds every `landed` and `captured` line, ticked and ended ones included; `--all-items`, the flag that already shows a todo's settled items, adds every other ticked and ended line; `--with-removed` keeps adding removed lines. Each fold count is exactly what its flag adds, so `--all-items` never reprints the hook history. MCP `show` takes `with_history` beside `all_items` and `with_removed`, and its JSON for `state` holds the same lines the text does, with the folded counts under `folded` (`history`, `settled`).
- **Hook line end:** a `landed` or `captured` line out of the newest 12 open ones and added over a day ago reads as ended, reason `out of the newest 12`, worked out at each read as other ends are; nothing is written. The brief, the summary of a seat's memory and ledger the memory hooks print at claim, session start and compaction, shows the newest 12 hook lines and a fold line for the rest; that fold line counts open lines only, so it shrinks with them, and its `ended since last brief` section never lists a hook line, which would otherwise list hundreds once a day passes.
- **The nudge** still counts every `landed` and `captured` line added since it that the agent has not settled, by the state the log records, so a line ending on its own never answers a nudge.

Why: on one seat `show state` printed 41 KB, 55% of it 206 open `landed` and `captured` lines and 39% 76 ticked or ended lines, beside 7 open agent lines; on another it printed 142,000 characters, past what an agent reads in one call. Git and the graph hold what a hook line points at, so an old one carries nothing a reader needs, and an open count of hundreds misstates what work is open. The default now costs about 3 KB on that seat, and every line stays one flag away.

Rejected:
- **Folding alone, hook lines open until ticked:** bounds the output, but the ledger keeps reporting hundreds of open lines nobody will act on.
- **Paging the whole ledger, newest first:** the first page still spends most of its tokens on ticked lines and hook history, and the live lines may need several pages.
- **A new `--with-settled` flag:** ledger lines are todolist items (20261028-223759-d-tac-jkj), and `--all-items` already shows a todo's settled items.

Accepted costs: one new flag and MCP key; the brief's fold line names `--with-history` instead of bare `show state`; a hook line nobody ticked leaves the open lines after a day.

Acceptance:

1. On a ledger with more than 12 open `landed` and `captured` lines and settled lines, `show state` prints the open agent lines, the newest 12 hook lines and both fold lines, and nothing settled.
2. `--with-history` and `--all-items` each add their group; MCP `show` with `with_history` and `all_items` returns the same lines as JSON.
3. A `landed` line out of the newest 12 and over a day old reads as ended; one under a day old stays open.
4. A nudge stays unanswered while a `landed` line added since it is unsettled, even once that line reads as ended.
5. The brief's fold line names `agentic-memory show state --with-history` and counts open lines only.
