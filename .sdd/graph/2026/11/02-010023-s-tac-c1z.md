Keys of agentic-memory's shown set no longer depend on the machine's time zone: commit 72a27d9 makes `presentedKey` digest an entry's line rendered in UTC, while every printed line keeps its local stamps. A zone change therefore reprints nothing, where before it made every entry read as changed and print in full once.

**Deviation found on the way.** The size-ledger failure of `TestEveryCommittedReleaseMigratesToWhatItHeld` on v2.25.0 turned on this digest, not on the operating system: `scripts/release-fixture.go` records with `TZ=UTC`, so a replay in another zone missed every key, wrote the slot and refreshed the ledger, hiding the stale ledger commit a8d810f fixed. Traces on macOS and in a Linux container under both zones showed it, and the test before a8d810f failed on macOS with `TZ=UTC`. The replay now behaves alike in every zone.

**Accepted cost.** Keys written in a non-UTC zone miss once after an upgrade, so those entries print in full one more time. Fixture keys, recorded in UTC, keep matching.

**Guard.** `TestAPresentedKeyHoldsAcrossZones` swaps `time.Local` for a filing time, a due time, a retirement and a supersede. Digesting in the machine's zone again failed all four cases. The change is not released yet.
