`agentic-memory` now offers the todo filing when a note, rule or trap is filed with a body listing steps, built in commit cad814df on branch `v2` (`feat: offer the ready todo filing when a note, rule or trap body lists three or more steps`), not yet released. It builds on the todolists of 20261028-223759-d-tac-jkj: a plan filed as a note has no step to tick, so the ledger never shows its progress.

A note, rule or trap whose body holds three or more consecutive list lines (an indented line continuing one keeps them consecutive, a blank line does not) is filed unchanged, and the reply adds `agentic-memory: this body lists steps; a todo ticks each one: --kind todo --item …`. Where every list line is a plain step (unindented, no `[x]`, no indented line continuing it; a `[ ] ` box drops), a ready command follows on a line of its own, filing the todo in place of the entry just filed, each list line an `--item`, every free-text word single-quoted for the shell; over MCP the offer is the matching `new` call. Lines never turn into items on their own, since not every list is a todolist. `docs/memory/usage.md` and the agent-memory skill name the new reply line.
