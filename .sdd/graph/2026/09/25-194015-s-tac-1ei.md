The grounding gate holds every graph write run through `Bash` to the turn's reads, and cleans up its own session ledgers.

As 20260925-192347-d-tac-sqt describes, an `agentic-capture` call passes on the turn's `--query` search, `--term` search and listing, or on a grounded draft: `agentic-grounding-gate record`, wired as a `PostToolUse` entry on `Write|Edit`, appends `{draft, graph}` to the `grounded` list in `.sdd/tmp/grounding-gate/<session>.json` after a save under a `drafts/` directory in a fully read turn, and the `turn` mode keeps that list. The graph state is the count of entry files under `.sdd/graph/` and the newest one's name.

As 20260925-193337-d-tac-9jv describes, a capture counts through runners, subshells, `bash -c`, `xargs` and any path, quoted or not, with the matching `sdd` reads use; a plain `sdd new` without `--dry-run` owes the reads with no grounded pass. A bare mention, including a script under `cmd/agentic-capture/`, stays free.

As 20260925-192325-d-tac-pc2 describes, `agentic-grounding-gate end` on `SessionEnd` removes a session's ledger and its agents' ledgers, and `agentic-grounding-gate start` on `SessionStart` removes session-named ledgers untouched for more than 7 days, keeping any other name.

Every acceptance criterion holds: the functional tests in `source/hooks/internal/grounding/grounding_test.go` cover each missing read, each spelling and each bare mention, the grounded carry-over and every way it is voided, and both cleanup modes; each new guard was broken, its test watched failing, and the file restored byte-identically. `go test -race ./...` passes in `source/hooks`, `agentic-install --print` shows the `PostToolUse` `Write|Edit`, `SessionStart` and `SessionEnd` entries, and `agentic-install` has wired them into `~/.claude/settings.json` with the binaries installed in `~/go/bin`. `README.md`, `skills/agent-grounding` and `skills/agent-capture` describe the behaviour.
