The grounding gate no longer refuses an `agentic-capture` of a draft carrying `kind: gap`. A gap draft is held to what every capture is held to: the turn's three reads or a grounded draft. `agentic-grounding-gate gate` loses the check and its `NO GAP CAPTURE` refusal (`refusals/gap.txt`), and the `agent-grounding` skill drops its "Never capture a gap" rule and the matching row of what the gate refuses.

Why: a gap is one of the graph's kinds, the record of a deviation that stays owed on the attention surface until a decision or a completed act resolves it. Refusing it outright left no way to record owed work that nobody can settle in the moment, so the problem lived only in a conversation or a notes file.

Rejected: keeping a softer rule in the skill that allows a gap only when neither a repair nor a settling decision is possible now. No hook can check that judgment, and the kind's own definition already says when a gap fits.

Accepted cost: an agent may capture a gap it could have repaired itself, and the append-only graph keeps it until a closing entry resolves it.

Acceptance: a capture of a `kind: gap` draft passes the gate once the turn carries every read; `TestEveryKindCaptures` covers it.
