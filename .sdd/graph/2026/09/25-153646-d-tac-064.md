The grounding gate holds graph reads to no wrapper. A bare `sdd search`, `sdd view` or `sdd show` passes in every project, and every refusal suggests plain `sdd` calls, with no `--entry` flag and no `ground` command. The stream and depth checks still see a call however it is spelled: in a subshell, after a runner such as `env` or `xargs`, by path, or inside `bash -c`.

Why: the rule held reads to `scripts/graph.py` because that wrapper recorded each read against its subject. It no longer records anything: format-d-fdbox's `graph.py` passes every read to `sdd` unchanged, and recording lives in sdd-autopilot. The gate therefore refused the bare reads fdbox's own `AGENTS.md` prescribes, and pointed at a flag `sdd` rejects and a command that does not exist.

Rejected: keeping the rule and correcting its wording. It would still refuse, for a recording that no longer happens, exactly the reads the project documents.

Accepted cost: detecting the tool behind a variable (`S=sdd; $S ...`) went with the rule, so a stream discarded through such an alias passes.

Settled: agentic-helpers commit a0a8f35 removes the rule, its refusal text and its alias detection, with tests covering every remaining call shape; the decision needs no follow-up work.
